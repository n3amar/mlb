WoW Forever crafting planner

A one-click bookmark for the classicwowforever.com auction house. It works for any profession, region and faction. It reads the live crafting prices for your auction house and gives you your top crafts plus one combined shopping list, with a max price for every material.

Install (one time)

  1. Click Copy bookmark code. If that doesn't copy, click in the box, then press Ctrl+A and Ctrl+C.
  2. Show your bookmarks bar with Ctrl+Shift+B (Mac: Cmd+Shift+B). Right-click it, choose Add page… and name it Craft Planner.
  3. Paste the code into the URL box and save. Pasting it into the address bar won't work.

Or drag this onto your bookmarks bar (this doesn't work in every browser): ⚒ Craft Planner

Use it

  1. Open the arbitrage tab. Pick your region, ruleset/faction and profession.
  2. Click the bookmark. A planner panel opens on the right.
  3. Untick recipes you don't know, then click Build my list.

How it decides

Only what you can make

Every recipe starts ticked. Open "Recipes I know" and untick the ones that aren't in your profession window, especially rare drop patterns. Your choices are saved per profession in your browser.

Ranked by real batch profit

Profit per craft × how many you'd actually make. The batch size is the site's "you could sell a day" × days of stock, capped by "Max per craft" (20 to start).

Safe confidence, real margins

Medium-confidence sales data or better by default. Low-confidence items often sell only a couple a day, or none. Crafts under a 20% margin are skipped, since one undercut can wipe out their profit. You can change this.

Max price, not target price

Every material shows the price the site used. Above that, the profit shrinks. "Craft yourself" means buy that item's own ingredients, like scraps for Light Leather or Heavy Hide + Salt for Cured Heavy Hide.

The planner uses whatever profession and house are selected on the site, so change those first, then click the bookmark. It includes cooldown crafts like Mooncloth only if the site's "Include cooldowns" box is ticked. Prices refresh about hourly on the site. If the site changes its layout, the bookmark may stop working until it's updated.
